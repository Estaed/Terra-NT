## Swipe between tabs (verdict)

**No — keep bottom-tab changes on taps.** A horizontal drag already pans Terra NT’s maps or reveals Saved’s delete action. Adding tab paging would give the same movement competing meanings. Result’s row dragging adds another gesture-sensitive area.

The published guidance supports that decision:

- **Material’s legacy bottom-navigation guidance:** “Using swipe gestures on the content area does not navigate between views.” [Material bottom navigation](https://m1.material.io/components/bottom-navigation.html). This is legacy guidance; an equivalent blanket prohibition in Material 3 was not verified.
- **Material’s separate tabs component:** “Use caution when placing other swipeable content (such as interactive maps or list items) in the content area.” [Material tabs](https://m2.material.io/components/tabs).
- **Apple HIG:** “A tab bar lets people navigate between top-level sections of your app.” Its gesture guidance says: “Use shortcut gestures to supplement standard gestures, not replace them.” Apple also requires custom gestures to be distinct. **Apple does not explicitly ban horizontal tab switching in the reviewed guidance**; avoiding it here is an application of its gesture principles. [Tab bars](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/tab-bars), [Gestures](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/gestures).

Comparable apps’ official instructions describe these interactions:

| App | Documented behavior |
|---|---|
| Google Maps | Tap **Explore** at the bottom; swipe **up** to reveal its content. [Google Maps Help](https://support.google.com/maps/answer/10014587?hl=en-419) |
| Apple Maps | Tap the **Map Modes** button and choose another map type. This supports button-based mode selection; it does not establish a comparable bottom-tab swipe pattern. [Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/set-up-maps-iph10d7bdf26/444) |
| AllTrails | Tap **Saved/Profile** in bottom navigation; swipe **up** on the navigation drawer. [AllTrails Help](https://support.alltrails.com/hc/en-us/articles/37228358315668-Using-the-Navigate-feature) |
| WikiCamps | Enter the **Plan** tab to create trips; drag the trip progress bar downward within a trip. [WikiCamps Help](https://support.wikicamps.co/hc/en-gb/articles/57933120336665-What-is-Trip-Planner-and-how-do-I-use-it) |
| Roadtrippers | Its iOS instructions explicitly say to tap the bottom **Map** tab; Android instructions use **Navigate** from Explore. [Roadtrippers Support](https://support.roadtrippers.com/hc/en-us/articles/208679216-How-can-I-preview-and-launch-GPS-directions-for-my-trip) |

These are documented interaction paths, **not device-tested proof that every current build rejects horizontal tab swipes**.
