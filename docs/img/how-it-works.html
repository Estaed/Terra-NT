How a trip gets planned

  1. 1PhoneYou answer about 15 questions and pick a start and end
  2. 2ServerChecks your sign-in and the answers
  3. 3ServerAdds its notes on NT places in your region
  4. 4ModelWrites a day-by-day plan in a fixed JSON shape
  5. 5ServerPuts every stop on the map, drops any outside the NT
  6. 6PhoneDraws the route; you edit it, save it, open it in Google Maps
