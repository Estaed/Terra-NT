## Access
Town on the sealed Stuart Highway. Airport with flights to Darwin, Tennant Creek and Alice Springs. Hire cars and taxis.

## Hours, fees, permits
No entry fee. Visitor Information Centre: 08 8972 2650, visitor@ktc.nt.gov.au.

## Seasons and heat
Dry season (May to Oct) is warm, sunny and busiest. Wet season (Nov to Apr) is hotter with storms; low season.

## Activities and swimming
Base for Nitmiluk Gorge, Leliyn (Edith Falls), Katherine Hot Springs and Elsey National Park. Fishing, walking and cycling trails, river cruises, scenic flights, art and museums.

## Stay
Hotels, motels and caravan parks in town; Cicada Lodge and Nitmiluk campground at the Gorge. Rates on operator sites.

## Wildlife and culture
Where Jawoyn, Dagoman and Wardaman lands meet. Locally guided cultural tours available.

## Fuel Station
About 13 outlets in Katherine: diesel, LAF, P95, P98; no unleaded 91 in town. LAF (Opal) is the substitute for unleaded 91. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
