Town on the Stuart Highway about 310 km south-east of Darwin, roughly 3.5 hours' drive via Adelaide River, Burrundie and Pine Creek. Katherine sits where the lands of the Jawoyn, Dagoman and Wardaman peoples meet. It is the base for Nitmiluk Gorge (about 30 km away) and Leliyn (Edith Falls, about 60 km north). Katherine Hot Springs is a short drive from town on the southern side of the Stuart Highway.
Cautions: fuel, opening hours and swimming status are not covered by this record. Check the NT Road Report, NT parks status and the BOM NT forecast before travelling, and confirm the hot springs are open and signed for swimming.
