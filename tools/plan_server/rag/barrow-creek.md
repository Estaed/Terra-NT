## Access
On the Stuart Highway, 282 km north of Alice Springs, against the Forster Ranges.

## Hours, fees, permits
Telegraph Station reserve open all year, no fee listed. Drone permit needed before arrival.

## Seasons and heat
Central Australian heat: avoid hard activity in the heat of the day; carry water, hat, sunscreen and insect repellent.

## Activities and swimming
Barrow Creek Telegraph Station: stone buildings from 1872, one of 15 original Overland Telegraph stations from Port Augusta to Darwin. Self-guided walk; info sheet from NT Parks or the Barrow Creek Pub. No swimming.

## Stay
No camping at the reserve. The Barrow Creek Pub is the local business; rates and fuel on enquiry.

## Wildlife and culture
Named by John McDouall Stuart in 1860. Historic ruins are protected. Respect Traditional Owners of the area.

## Fuel Station
Barrow Creek Hotel: diesel and P95 only, no unleaded 91 or LAF. Next south: Ti Tree, about 80 km. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
