## Access
135 km west of Alice Springs on sealed roads (Larapinta and Namatjira Drives). Drive-in, caravan suitable.

## Hours, fees, permits
NT Parks Visitor Pass. Drone permit before arrival. Kiosk has seasonal hours.

## Seasons and heat
Over 40 C possible October to April; dry heat causes exhaustion. Check BOM warnings.

## Activities and swimming
Permanent waterhole 500 m from car park, up to 14 m deep and very cold: hypothermia risk, no jumping or diving, watch for logs. Ormiston Pound loop 8.5 km, 3-4 h; may need a short swim through cold water. Larapinta Trail sections 9 and 10 start here.

## Stay
Category B campground ($10/adult/night), booked online.

Glen Helen, about 10 km west: diesel, P95; pumps can be out of order, then Alice Springs is about 135 km. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
