Permanent waterhole and gorge in Tjoritja / West MacDonnell National Park, about 135 km west of Alice Springs (NT Parks fact sheet). The Aranda people call the ranges Tjoritja. Ormiston Gorge is the start of Sections 9 and 10 of the Larapinta Trail and the base for the Ormiston Pound Walk (8.5 km, 3 to 4 hours per the fact sheet) and the Mount Giles overnight walk (Grade 5, difficult). A campground is available. A parks pass applies to NT visitors.
Cautions: temperatures can exceed 40 C between October and April. The status page has listed the Ormiston Gorge kiosk as closed, and the park can close for fire, flood or maintenance. Check status, and swim only where signage and status allow.
