## Access
Off the Stuart Highway, 100 km south of Tennant Creek.

## Hours, fees, permits
Open all year. NT Parks Visitor Pass for non-residents ($10 adult/day). Camping booked and paid online only.

## Seasons and heat
Can exceed 40 C October to April. Check BOM heatwave warnings, carry plenty of water, avoid hard activity in the heat of the day.

## Activities and swimming
Granite boulder fields with short walking tracks and picnic tables. No swimming.

## Stay
Campground with caravan sites; host on site May to Sep. NT Parks rates $10-$20/adult/night.

## Wildlife and culture
Sacred site of the Kaytete, Warumungu, Warlpiri and Alyawarra people; handed back in 2008 and jointly managed. Traditional Owners ask that you do not climb the karlu (boulders). Cultural items and wildlife are protected.

## Fuel Station
Devils Marbles Hotel at Wauchope, about 10 km south: diesel, LAF. Tennant Creek about 100 km north. Do not count on Wycliffe Well. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
