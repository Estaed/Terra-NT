Palm-fringed thermal pool in Elsey National Park, near Mataranka and about 120 km south of Katherine. NT Parks says the thermal waters here and at Bitter Springs stay a warm 34 C year round. Reach the pool via Homestead Road; Bitter Springs is via Martin Road. A path runs from the car park to the pool, with stairs and handrails into the water, and a 500 m loop walk. Camping is at Jalmurark Campground, not at Bitter Springs. A parks pass is required.
Cautions: riverside walks have been closed for seasonal conditions on the official status page, and sites can close for fire or flooding. Check the Elsey National Park status page and NT Road Report before you go, and swim only where signage and status allow.
