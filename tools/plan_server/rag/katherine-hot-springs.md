## Access
In Katherine, beside the Katherine River. Sealed road. Boardwalk with wheelchair access to the main pool.

## Hours, fees, permits
Open daily 7am to 7pm. No park pass needed. No alcohol at the springs.

## Seasons and heat
Closed to swimmers in the wet season (about October to April) when the river rises or floods. Water is around 25 to 30 C all year.

## Activities and swimming
Swimming in natural thermal pools linked by boardwalk. No lifeguard: swim at your own risk and supervise children.

## Stay
No camping on site. Hotels, motels and caravan parks in Katherine.

## Wildlife and culture
Traditional Owners acknowledged by Katherine Town Council; Katherine sits on Jawoyn, Dagoman and Wardaman lands.

## Fuel Station
United Katherine South on the Victoria Hwy, about 1 km: diesel, LAF, P95. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
