Natural thermal pools on the banks of the Katherine River, about 2 km from Katherine CBD via Victoria Highway / Riverbank Drive. Series of clear pools at roughly 25–30 °C framed by pandanus and paperbark. Wheelchair-accessible main pool with ramp and handrails. Picnic area, playground and seasonal café. Free entry. Open daily 7am–7pm in the dry season.
Cautions: closed to swimming during the wet season (typically October–April) when the Katherine River rises. Swimming is at your own risk; no lifeguard. Always check current status with Katherine Town Council or local signage.
