## Access
On Larapinta Drive, 7 km west of central Alice Springs. Wheelchair accessible; electric scooters available.

## Hours, fees, permits
Daily 7:30am to 6pm, last entry 4:30pm. Closed Christmas Day. Entry fees on the Desert Park website.

## Seasons and heat
Outdoor park with no heat rule listed; plan around midday heat.

## Activities and swimming
Walking tracks through three habitats: sand country, desert rivers, woodland. Nature Theatre free-flying birds of prey show. Nocturnal House (bilby, mala, thorny devil). Nocturnal spotlight tour. One-hour guided foothills tour. No swimming.

## Stay
Day visit only; stay in Alice Springs.

## Wildlife and culture
Rare desert mammals, reptiles and birds. Daily cultural presentations.

## Fuel Station
Larapinta Dr outlets in Alice Springs, about 5-7 km: LAF and P95; regular diesel at Shell Mt Gillen. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
