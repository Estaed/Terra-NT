Park on Larapinta Drive, 7 km from the centre of Alice Springs, described by NT Parks as the gateway to the West MacDonnell Ranges. Three habitats (sand country, desert rivers and woodland) hold native plants and wildlife, including bilby, mala and thorny devil in the Nocturnal House. Highlights are the Nature Theatre free-flying birds of prey show, daily guided activities and cultural presentations, and a nocturnal spotlight tour. There is a cafe, gift shop and picnic ground. Tourism NT lists opening daily 7:30am to 6pm, with closure on 25 December 2026.
Cautions: confirm hours, tour times and entry costs on the Alice Springs Desert Park website before visiting.
