## Access
450 km south-west of Alice Springs. Stuart Hwy, Lasseter Hwy and Luritja Rd are sealed (2WD). Ernest Giles Rd and the Mereenie Loop are unsealed: 4WD recommended, permit for the Loop.

## Hours, fees, permits
NT Parks Visitor Pass.

## Seasons and heat
Hot months Sep to Apr; heat strategy applies. At 36 C or more forecast: Rim Walk must start before 9am, South Wall before 11am.

## Activities and swimming
Rim Walk 6 km loop, 3-4 h, grade 4, clockwise, steep start. South Wall 4.8 km return, 2 h, moderate. Kings Creek 2.6 km return, 1 h, easy. Kathleen Springs (21 km away) 2.4 km return, easy, wheelchair access, no swimming.

## Stay
No camping inside the park. Kings Canyon Resort (rooms, camping, bunkhouse) and Kings Creek Station (camping, safari cabins).

## Wildlife and culture
Kathleen Springs waterhole is significant to local Aboriginal people.

## Fuel Station
Kings Canyon Resort, about 10 km: diesel, ULP95. Kings Creek Station, 36 km, closes in the wet season. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
