## Access
Entry from Yulara. Kata Tjuta is about 50 km from Uluru.

## Hours, fees, permits
Open daily, sunrise to after sunset (e.g. 5am-9pm Dec-Feb, 6:30am-7:30pm Jun-Jul); leave by closing. Own pass: adult $38 for 3 days, under 18 free; NT vehicle annual $109. NT pass and Kakadu pass not valid.

## Seasons and heat
At 36 C or more, Valley of the Winds closes beyond Karu lookout from 11am. Start long walks early.

## Activities and swimming
Uluru base walk 10.6 km, 3.5 h, grade 3. Mala walk 2 km, 1.5 h, easy. Valley of the Winds 7.4 km, 4 h, grade 4. Walpa Gorge 2.6 km, 1 h. No swimming.

## Stay
No camping in the park; stay at Yulara.

## Wildlife and culture
Aṉangu country (Yankunytjatjara, Pitjantjatjara), co-managed. Climbing Uluru is banned. Do not photograph sensitive sites. Kata Tjuta is a sacred men's site.

## Fuel Station
Shell at Ayers Rock Resort, Yulara, about 20 km: diesel, LAF, P98. Open 7am to 8pm. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
