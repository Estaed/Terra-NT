## Access
Bowali Visitor Centre at Jabiru. Main roads sealed; remote sites 4WD or dry season only.

## Hours, fees, permits
Own Parks Australia pass, 7 days (NT pass not valid). Dry season adult $40, family $100; tropical summer adult $25. Free for NT residents. Buy online or at Bowali.

## Seasons and heat
Dry season April to October; tropical summer November to March. Sites close at short notice.

## Activities and swimming
Rock art, cruises, waterfalls, ranger talks. Saltwater crocs present; obey signs. Safest swim is a hotel pool.

## Stay
Campgrounds and bush camps. Hotels and cabins in Jabiru and Cooinda. Rates on operator/Parks Australia pages.

## Wildlife and culture
Bininj/Mungguy country, jointly managed. Keep out of sacred and burial sites. No photos of people without permission.

## Fuel Station
In the park: Ausfuel Jabiru (diesel, LAF, P95, P98) and Cooinda Lodge (diesel, LAF, P95). Western edge: Bark Hut Inn and Corroboree Park (diesel, P95, U91). South: Pine Creek. No fuel at South Alligator, Mary River or the Border Store. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
