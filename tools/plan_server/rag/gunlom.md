## Access
South Kakadu, Mary River region. Gunlom Road is gravel; high-clearance 4WD only.

## Hours, fees, permits
Kakadu park pass required. Check the Kakadu Access Report before going.

## Seasons and heat
Closes after heavy rain. Best in the dry season.

## Activities and swimming
85 m waterfall. Climb to the top: 2 km return, steep, natural infinity pool and lookout. Murrill Billabong walk 2.5 km return. Strictly no access or swimming in the bottom plunge pool. Follow signs at the top pools; be Crocwise near all water.

## Stay
Gunlom campground (managed, $15/adult/night).

## Wildlife and culture
Bininj/Mungguy country (Jawoyn: Mungguy). Areas may close at Traditional Owner request.

## Fuel Station
No fuel near Gunlom; Mary River Roadhouse no longer sells fuel. Pine Creek, about 105 km (last 36 km unsealed): diesel, LAF, P95. Fill up and carry spare. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
