## Access
138 km west of Alice Springs; turn south off Larapinta Drive just west of Hermannsburg. Last 16 km follows the sandy Finke River bed: high-clearance 4WD only. Impassable after heavy rain.

## Hours, fees, permits
Open all year. NT Parks Visitor Pass. Camping booked and paid online only. No pets. Drone permit before arrival.

## Seasons and heat
Best April to September. Over 40 C possible October to April.

## Activities and swimming
Walks: Kalarranga, Mparra, Arankaia and Mpulungkinya. Scenic lookouts. No swimming listed.

## Stay
Palm Valley campground and Finke River sites at Boggy Hole; NT Parks rates $10-$20/adult/night. Host May to Sep.

## Wildlife and culture
Near Hermannsburg, linked to artist Albert Namatjira.

## Fuel Station
Ntaria Supermarket in Hermannsburg, about 20 km: diesel, LAF. Check store hours. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
