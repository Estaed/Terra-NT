## Access
Sealed, 68 km east of Darwin: Stuart and Arnhem Highways, then 10 km on Anzac Parade. 2WD, all year.

## Hours, fees, permits
Open all year. NT Parks Visitor Pass. No drones.

## Seasons and heat
Lilies bloom Dec-Jul; most bird variety Jul-Dec. Mosquitoes bad at sunrise and sunset. Avoid midday exertion.

## Activities and swimming
Woodlands to Waterlilies 2.2 km return, 45 min, grade 2. Monsoon Forest Walk 4.4 km return, 1.5 hr, grade 2. Saltwater crocodiles: no swimming, do not walk on the dam wall.

## Stay
No camping in the reserve; stay in Humpty Doo or Darwin.

## Wildlife and culture
Limilngan-Wulna people speak for this land and help manage it. Nearly 200 bird species, water pythons, turtles.

## Fuel Station
Humpty Doo, about 27-30 km: S24 (U91, P95, P98, premium diesel); regular diesel at United Humpty Doo. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
