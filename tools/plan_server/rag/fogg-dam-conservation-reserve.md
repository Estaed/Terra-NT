Wetland reserve about 70 km south-east of Darwin via sealed roads off the Arnhem Highway (turn onto Anzac Parade). One of the most accessible Top End wetlands for year-round birdwatching and wildlife viewing. Short walks include the Monsoon Walk and Woodlands to Water Lilies Walk; dam-wall lookouts available. Traditional lands of the Limilngan-Wulna people. NT Parks Visitor Pass required.
Cautions: crocodiles are present. Stay on designated paths and observe all signage. Some walks may close after flooding. Check the official park status page and NT Road Report before visiting.
