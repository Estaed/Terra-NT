## Access
Area (falls, Twin Falls and Karnamarr campground) closed for road upgrades until Parks Australia reopens it; do not plan it without checking. 4WD only, dry season only. Turnoff 43 km south of Bowali on Kakadu Hwy, then about 50 km track; last 10 km soft sand. Twin Falls needs a high-clearance 4WD with snorkel (deep Jim Jim Creek crossing).

## Hours, fees, permits
Kakadu Park Pass required. Twin Falls boat ticket from Bowali or Karnamarr. Check Access Report.

## Seasons and heat
Wet season: air access only. Falls stop flowing in the dry.

## Activities and swimming
Jim Jim plunge pool walk 2 km return. Twin Falls gorge via shuttle boat plus walk. No swimming at Twin Falls. Be Crocwise.

## Stay
Karnamarr campground (managed). Jim Jim Billabong (unmanaged). Rates on Parks Australia pages.

## Wildlife and culture
Bininj/Mungguy country. Respect closures and cultural requests.

## Fuel Station
Cooinda Lodge Kakadu, about 70 km: diesel, LAF, P95. Jabiru is further. Fill up before the 4WD track. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
