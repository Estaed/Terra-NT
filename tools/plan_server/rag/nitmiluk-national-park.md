On the lands of the Jawoyn people, with rock art sites across the park; Nitmiluk is the Jawoyn name for Katherine Gorge. About 320 km south of Darwin, in two sections: the Gorge entrance is 30 km north-east of Katherine on a sealed road, and Leliyn (Edith Falls) is 60 km north. The Nitmiluk Visitor Centre at the Gorge entrance is the suggested first stop. Walks, gorge boat tours and the Jatbula Trail are available. A parks pass is required for NT visitors.
Cautions: temperatures can exceed 40 C between October and April. The park can close due to bushfire, flooding or maintenance, and NT Parks advises checking which swimming spots are open. Do not assume the gorge is safe to swim in; follow signage and the official status page.
