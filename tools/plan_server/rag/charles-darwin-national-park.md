## Access
Sealed, 5.5 km from Darwin city via Tiger Brennan Drive; entry at Winnellie opposite Bowen Road. Vehicles on the one-way main roads only.

## Hours, fees, permits
Daily 8am to 7pm, day use only. Free entry, WWII display included. No drones.

## Seasons and heat
Open all year. Midges peak at sunrise, sunset and full moons Aug-Oct; wear repellent. Avoid midday exertion.

## Activities and swimming
Harbour lookout, stairs down to the mangroves, mountain bike trails, WWII bunkers. No swimming.

## Stay
No camping; stay in Darwin.

## Wildlife and culture
Larrakia people speak for this land; shell middens show long use. 36 of the NT's 51 mangrove species.

## Fuel Station
Winnellie, about 4 km: U91, P95, diesel; fuel widely available in Darwin. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
