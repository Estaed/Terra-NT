Small national park only 5.5 km from Darwin city centre on Tiger Brennan Drive (entry at Winnellie opposite Bowen Road). Protects part of the Port Darwin wetland with 36 of the Territory’s 51 mangrove species, woodland, Aboriginal shell middens used by Larrakia people, and World War II ammunition bunkers with a display. Lookout platform offers views of the city and harbour. Mountain-bike trails, bushwalking, picnicking and wildlife watching. Open daily 8am–7pm.
Cautions: check current park status. Wear protective clothing and insect repellent. No camping.
