Watarrka National Park includes the western end of the George Gill Range, and Kings Canyon is a highlight of Central Australia. Kathleen Springs is 21 km by road from Kings Canyon. The Kings Canyon Rim Walk must be done clockwise and starts with a difficult climb, so it suits fit, healthy walkers. Shorter options are the Kings Creek Walk and the South Wall Return Walk; the Garden of Eden cannot be reached from the South Wall Return Walk. A parks pass is required for NT visitors.
Cautions: when the forecast is 36 C or above, the Rim Walk must start before 9am, and no start is allowed after 9am. A heat management strategy applies. A Mereenie Loop Pass is needed on Larapinta Drive between Hermannsburg or Glen Helen and Watarrka. Check park status and the BOM forecast for Watarrka.
