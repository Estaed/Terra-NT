## Access
On the Stuart Highway, about 200 km south of Alice Springs and 268 km east of Uluru and Kata Tjuta.

## Hours, fees, permits
No entry fee. Contact 08 8956 0984 or admin@erldundaroadhouse.com.

## Seasons and heat
Central Australian climate: hot summers, cold desert nights in winter.

## Activities and swimming
Overnight stop before Uluru or Alice Springs. Day trips to Uluru on selected days. Swimming pool, playground and tennis court at the resort.

## Stay
Erldunda Desert Oaks Resort: grassy campground; listed from $15 a night. Pets by enquiry.

## Wildlife and culture
Stop on the route between Alice Springs and Uluru.

## Fuel Station
Erldunda Desert Oaks: diesel, LAF, P98. Next: Curtin Springs about 100 km west, Stuarts Well about 108 km north. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
