## Access
Just off the Stuart Highway near its junction with the Carpentaria Highway, 274 km south of Katherine.

## Hours, fees, permits
No entry fee. Aviation hangar display open to visitors.

## Seasons and heat
Drier than the Top End coast. April to October is cooler; November to March is hotter with more chance of rain.

## Activities and swimming
Daly Waters Pub (built 1930, one of the NT's oldest buildings). Aviation complex: 1926 air race stop, WWII air force base, Qantas refuel stop. Stuart's Tree, marked 'S' by John McDouall Stuart in 1862. Annual rodeo. Frew Ponds Overland Telegraph memorial further south. No swimming spots listed.

## Stay
Pub accommodation from safari tents to cabins. Rates on operator site.

## Wildlife and culture
Jingili country; Emu and Sun Dreaming tracks pass through the area.

## Fuel Station
Daly Waters Pub: diesel, LAF, P95. Hi-Way Inn at the Carpentaria Hwy junction, about 5 km: diesel, LAF. Next south: Dunmarra. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
