Town in Australia's Red Centre. Its Aboriginal name is Mparntwe, and the Traditional Custodians are the Arrernte people, known for the Yeperenye (caterpillar) story. Aboriginal art galleries line the Desert Art Trail. Alice Springs Airport is 10 minutes from the town centre, QantasLink flies in from several capitals including Darwin, and the Ghan train runs from Adelaide to Alice Springs and Darwin. Three bus routes run Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays. Spring and autumn are the best times to visit; December to February is low season.
