## Access
Stuart Highway town in the Red Centre. Airport about 10 minutes from town: QantasLink from Darwin and major capitals. The Ghan stops between Adelaide and Darwin. Three bus routes Mon to Sat; car, bike or foot is easiest.

## Hours, fees, permits
No entry fee. Visitor Information Centre (Todd Mall and Parsons St) sells Mereenie Loop permits.

## Seasons and heat
High season spring (Sep to Nov) and autumn (Mar to May): warm days, cool nights. Summer (Dec to Feb) is hot, low season.

## Activities and swimming
Desert Art Trail galleries, camel rides, sunrise balloon flights, Desert Park, events. Base for West MacDonnell Ranges, Kings Canyon and Uluru trips.

## Stay
Range of accommodation. Rates on operator sites.

## Wildlife and culture
Arrernte country (Mparntwe). Yeperenye (caterpillar) story shapes the landscape.

## Fuel Station
Fuel widely available in Alice Springs, some outlets 24/7. Fill up before heading west or south. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
