## Access
Elsey National Park, off the Stuart Highway at Mataranka via Homestead Road. Park is 8 km from Mataranka. Path from car park to pool is wheelchair accessible.

## Hours, fees, permits
NT Parks Visitor Pass. No set hours listed. Park can close for fire, flood or works.

## Seasons and heat
Water about 34 C all year. Air over 40 C possible October to April. Riverside walks can close seasonally.

## Activities and swimming
Thermal pool swim with stairs and handrails. Pool loop 500 m, 20 min, easy. Stevie's Hole 1.2 km return, 45 min, easy. Riverside Walk 16 km, 8 h, easy. Roper River fishing and two boat ramps.

## Stay
Jalmurark Campground, 16 km away: NT Parks rates $10-$20/adult/night; hosts May to Sep.

## Wildlife and culture
Palm-fringed spring-fed pool. WWII Aboriginal Army Camp walk.

## Fuel Station
Mataranka town, about 8 km from the thermal pool: diesel and P95; LAF only at Mataranka Central. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
