## Access
On the Stuart Highway in the Barkly Tablelands, between Alice Springs and Darwin; usual overnight stop. Airport with flights to Katherine. Town is walkable.

## Hours, fees, permits
No entry fee. Davenport Range National Park to the south-east is 4WD only.

## Seasons and heat
April to October is cooler; nights can be cold. November to March is hot with more chance of rain. Less flooding than the Top End.

## Activities and swimming
Nyinkka Nyunyu Art and Culture Centre (Aboriginal owned). Battery Hill Mining Centre, under 2 km from town: mine tours and gold panning. Lake Mary Ann (Tingkkarli), 5 km north: swimming, fishing, canoeing. Telegraph Station 12 km north. Karlu Karlu 100 km south.

## Stay
Plenty of lodgings in town. Rates on operator sites.
