## Access
Small town (about 250 people) on the Stuart Highway, 114 km south of Darwin. Common stop between Darwin and Katherine.

## Hours, fees, permits
No entry fee. War cemetery open to visitors.

## Seasons and heat
Dry season May to Oct busiest; wet Nov to Apr brings heat and storms.

## Activities and swimming
Adelaide River War Cemetery: 434 servicemen and 63 civilians from WWII, including Post Office staff killed in the first Darwin air raid. Old railway bridge and 1889 station, now a museum. Start or end of the Northern Goldfields Loop heritage drive to Pine Creek. No swimming spot listed; assume any Top End water may hold crocodiles.

## Stay
Adelaide River Inn (pub, cabins, caravan sites). Rates on operator site.

## Wildlife and culture
Major WWII military headquarters site. Larrakia and other groups.

## Fuel Station
Adelaide River Mobil and Shell on the Stuart Hwy: diesel, LAF, P95; no unleaded 91. LAF (Opal) is the substitute for unleaded 91. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
