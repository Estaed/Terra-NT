## Access
NT capital. Darwin International Airport is 7 km from the city; hire cars, taxis, rideshare and shuttles. Compact city with walking and cycle paths; buses stop at tourist spots. Hire a car for trips out of town.

## Hours, fees, permits
No entry fee. Some tours do not run in the wet; check ahead.

## Seasons and heat
Dry season May to October is high season with most events. Wet season brings evening storms, greenery and fewer crowds.

## Activities and swimming
Mindil Beach Sunset Markets (April to October), night and Asian food markets, sunset cruises, croc encounters, open-air cinema, galleries and museums, barramundi fishing. Box jellyfish risk in the sea, mainly 1 Oct to 1 Jun; assume any natural water may hold crocodiles.

## Stay
Full range of accommodation. Rates on operator sites.

## Wildlife and culture
Larrakia country (Garramilla); Larrakia observe seven seasons. Aboriginal art across galleries and streets.
