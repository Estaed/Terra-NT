Capital of the Northern Territory, on Larrakia Country. The Larrakia people are the Traditional Custodians of Darwin/Garramilla and observe seven seasons. Aboriginal art fills the city's galleries, museums and streetscapes, and there are night markets and a food scene with strong Asian influences. Darwin International Airport is 7 km from the city. It is the smallest Australian capital, so many attractions can be reached on foot or by bike, and buses stop at popular tourist spots. Dry season (May to October) is high season; the wet season brings storms and fewer crowds.
Cautions: weather and conditions change by season. Check the BOM NT forecast and the NT Road Report before driving out of town.
