## Access
Sealed 2WD, 41 km from Jabiru. Rain can cut the road Dec-Apr; check the Kakadu Access Report.

## Hours, fees, permits
Apr-Nov 8.30am to sunset; Dec-Mar 2pm to sunset (check before you go). Kakadu park pass.

## Seasons and heat
Best road access May-Oct. Sunset at the lookout is the busiest time.

## Activities and swimming
Art walk 1 km loop, 1 hr, grade 2; lookout climb 250 m, steep, grade 3. No swimming; crocodiles: never walk on Cahills Crossing.

## Stay
Merl campground, 3 km: $15 adult, $7.50 child, $38 family a night, not bookable.

## Wildlife and culture
Bininj/Mungguy country. No alcohol at Ubirr; ask before photographing people. Rock wallabies at dusk.

## Fuel Station
Ausfuel Jabiru, about 41 km: diesel, LAF, P95, P98. No fuel at the Border Store. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
