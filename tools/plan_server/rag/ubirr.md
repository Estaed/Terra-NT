Major Aboriginal rock-art site in the East Alligator region of Kakadu National Park, about 40 km from Jabiru via sealed road. Short circular walk (about 1 km) past galleries depicting ancestral figures and local animals; a further climb reaches a lookout over the Nadab floodplain (popular at sunset). Requires a Kakadu park pass. Ranger-guided walks are available.
Cautions: access can be restricted in the wet season. Check the Kakadu Access Report before recommending the site. Alcohol is not permitted for cultural reasons. Swim only where official signage and status allow.
