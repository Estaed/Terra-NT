## Access
Resort town outside Uluru-Kata Tjuta National Park, about 20 km from Uluru and 50 km from Kata Tjuta.

## Hours, fees, permits
No fee for the town. Park pass needed to enter the national park.

## Seasons and heat
Hot summers; plan outdoor activity early or late in the day.

## Activities and swimming
Base for Uluru and Kata Tjuta. Pool and playground at the campground.

## Stay
Hotels and lodges: Sails in the Desert, Desert Gardens, The Lost Camel, Outback Hotel and Lodge, Emu Walk Apartments, Longitude 131. Ayers Rock Campground: powered and unpowered sites and air-con cabins. Curtin Springs also has camping. Rates on operator sites. No camping inside the park.

## Wildlife and culture
On the edge of Aṉangu country. No takeaway alcohol sales at the campground.

## Fuel Station
Shell Ayers Rock Resort: diesel, LAF, P98, open 7am to 8pm. Next: Curtin Springs, 85 km east. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
