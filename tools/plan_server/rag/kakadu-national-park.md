Dual-listed UNESCO World Heritage park of about 20,000 sq km, with world-class rock art, wetlands and Stone Country. Its western boundary is about 150 km east of Darwin; Bowali Visitor Centre near Jabiru is 250 km from Darwin. The park runs nearly 200 km north to south and over 100 km east to west, so it should not be crammed into a day. Traditional Owners recognise six seasons; for planning, Parks Australia uses the dry season (April to October) and tropical summer (November to March). A park pass is required and is bought through Parks Australia; extra NT permits may apply for fishing and camping.
Cautions: do not assume all of Kakadu is reachable by standard car. Some remote attractions use rough or 4WD roads. Check the Kakadu Access Report and NT Road Report before recommending a site, and swim only where signage and official status allow.
