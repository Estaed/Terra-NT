Permanent billabong in southern Kakadu near Cooinda, known as Ngurrungurrudjba. Excellent wildlife viewing (crocodiles, birds, buffalo and more) via boardwalk or commercial Yellow Water Cruises operated by an Indigenous business. Base for the nearby Warradjan Aboriginal Cultural Centre and accommodation/camping options. Requires a Kakadu park pass.
Cautions: check the Kakadu Access Report and current cruise operating conditions. Swim only where officially permitted and signed. Conditions change with the seasons.
