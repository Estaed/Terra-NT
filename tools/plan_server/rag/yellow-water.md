## Access
Wetland at Cooinda, central Kakadu. Cooinda campground is 2WD accessible.

## Hours, fees, permits
Kakadu park pass required. Cruises are booked with the operator (yellowwater.com.au, 08 8979 1500).

## Seasons and heat
Cruises run all year. Floodplain walk opens once waters recede. Barramundi Feb to Nov.

## Activities and swimming
Nature and culture boat cruise. Boardwalk for wildlife viewing. Home Billabong floodplain walk 2.6 km return. No swimming: saltwater crocodiles. Warradjan Aboriginal Cultural Centre nearby, open daily.

## Stay
Cooinda Lodge: motel and budget rooms, 100 powered and 200 unpowered sites. Mardukal campgrounds 6 km away: $15/adult/night.

## Wildlife and culture
Saltwater crocs, wetland birds. Warradjan was built by Bininj/Mungguy; no photos inside.

## Fuel Station
Cooinda Lodge Kakadu beside the billabong: diesel, LAF, P95. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
