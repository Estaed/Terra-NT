Major World Heritage rock-art site in Kakadu National Park (Burrungkuy region). 1.5 km circuit walk through galleries including Anbangbang shelter with paintings of Namarrkon (Lightning Man) and other ancestral figures spanning thousands of years. Kunwarddewardde lookout offers views of the Arnhem Land escarpment. Nearby Anbangbang Billabong and Nawurlandja lookout. Requires Kakadu park pass. Sealed road access from Jabiru area.
Cautions: check Kakadu Access Report for current road and site status. Gates typically 7am–7pm. Ranger-guided walks available in dry season. Swim only where official signage permits.
