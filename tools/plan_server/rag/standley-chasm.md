## Access
In the West MacDonnell Ranges, reached from Alice Springs via Larapinta Drive. Gates locked after trading hours.

## Hours, fees, permits
Mon to Sat 8am to 5pm, Sun 8am to 2pm. Privately run entry fee: adult $12, child $5-$7, family $30, Alice locals $1 with ID. NT Parks pass does not cover it. Sells Mereenie Loop permits.

## Seasons and heat
Over 40 C possible October to April; walk early.

## Activities and swimming
Creek-bed walk about 1.2 km each way (about 15 min) to an 80 m sheer rock chasm, with seats and signs. Aboriginal guided tours. No swimming.

## Stay
Campground: 6 powered sites, unpowered and tent areas; $25.50 per person a night. Entry included with camping.

## Wildlife and culture
Aboriginal-owned; important Arrernte cultural site. Respect the protected site.

## Fuel Station
No fuel at the chasm. Shell Mt Gillen on Larapinta Dr, Alice Springs, about 50 km: diesel, LAF, P95. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
