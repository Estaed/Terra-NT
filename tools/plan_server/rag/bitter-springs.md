## Access
Sealed Martin Road from Mataranka (Stuart Hwy). 2WD suitable. Path from car park to water is pram and wheelchair accessible. Check NT Road Report for wet-season flooding on access roads.

## Hours, fees, permits
NT Parks Visitor Pass required (buy online). No set hours. Park can close for fire, flood or works; check official status page.

## Seasons and heat
Thermal water about 34 C all year. Air over 40 C possible October to April.

## Activities and swimming
Swimming in the thermal waterhole; stairs with handrails into the water. Loop walk 500 m, 20 min, grade 1 easy. Picnic area.

## Stay
No camping at Bitter Springs. Jalmurark Campground in Elsey National Park is 22 km away.

## Wildlife and culture
Thermal waterhole in Elsey National Park. Follow park signage.

## Fuel Station
Mataranka, about 3 km: diesel and P95 at all three outlets; LAF only at Mataranka Central. No fuel at the springs. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
