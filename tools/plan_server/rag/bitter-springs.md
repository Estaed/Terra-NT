Companion thermal pool to Mataranka Thermal Pool in Elsey National Park, near Mataranka. Crystal-clear spring water stays about 34 C year-round. Accessed via Martin Road from the northern end of Mataranka. Short boardwalk and 500 m loop walk through Livistona palms; stairs with handrails into the water. NT Parks Visitor Pass required. No camping at the site (use Jalmurark Campground).
Cautions: swimming status and facilities can change; toilets were noted for upgrade works. Follow on-site signage and check the official Elsey National Park status page before visiting. Crocodile risk exists outside designated swimming areas.
