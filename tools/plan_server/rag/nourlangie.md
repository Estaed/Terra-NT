## Access
Rock art site in east Kakadu, south of Jabiru. Main gallery walk is wheelchair accessible.

## Hours, fees, permits
Gates 7am to 7pm daily. Kakadu park pass required.

## Seasons and heat
Main gallery walk open all year. Guided rock art activities run in the dry season.

## Activities and swimming
Burrungkuy walk: 1.5 km loop, about 2 hours, grade 3, past Anbangbang shelter and galleries. Moderately steep climb to Kunwarddewardde lookout. Ranger talks: ask at Bowali. No swimming here.

## Stay
No camping at the site. Hotels, cabins and campsites in Jabiru.

## Wildlife and culture
Bininj/Mungguy country. Art includes Namarrkon (lightning man), x-ray animals and contact-era ships. Older art is often painted over; do not touch art or enter closed areas.

## Fuel Station
Ausfuel Jabiru, about 31 km: diesel, LAF, P95, P98. Cooinda Lodge is the alternative. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
