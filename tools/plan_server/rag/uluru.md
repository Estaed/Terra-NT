Hours: open every day of the year and closed at night; every visitor must leave by
closing time. The hours change by month: 5:00am–9:00pm in December, January and
February; 5:30am–8:30pm in March; 5:30am–8:00pm in April; 6:00am–7:30pm in May and
August; 6:30am–7:30pm in June and July; 5:30am–7:30pm in September; 5:00am–8:00pm in
October; 5:00am–8:30pm in November. The Cultural Centre is open 7:00am–5:45pm daily.

Fees: a park pass is required. Adults (18 and over) $38 for 3 days or $50 for an annual
pass; children and teenagers under 18 enter free. Buy online before arriving, or at the
entry station. The pass covers this park only; Kakadu needs its own pass.

Respect: the Uluru climb is permanently closed. Never suggest climbing Uluru. Use the dual
name Uluru-Kata Tjuta National Park.

What to do:
- Sunrise and sunset at the designated viewing areas (Talinguru Nyakunytjaku for sunrise).
- Uluru base walk, 10.6 km around the rock; allow about 3.5 hours and start in the cool
  of the morning.
- Walpa Gorge walk and the Valley of the Winds walks at Kata Tjuta.
- Cultural Centre: Anangu culture, galleries and the Ininti cafe.

Stay: hotels, apartments and the campground at Ayers Rock Resort in Yulara, just outside
the park.
