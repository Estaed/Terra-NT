## Access
On the Stuart Highway, about 90 km north of Katherine.

## Hours, fees, permits
No entry fee to town. Umbrawarra Gorge Nature Park needs an NT Parks Visitor Pass.

## Seasons and heat
Dry season May to Oct is warm and busy; wet Nov to Apr is hotter with storms.

## Activities and swimming
1870s gold rush town: Repeater Museum, Old Playford Club Hotel (1889), Railway Heritage Precinct, Brocks Creek Chinatown and Temple site. Northern Goldfields Loop heritage drive to Adelaide River. Lake Copperfield, about 6 km south, for swimming and picnics. Umbrawarra Gorge: red cliffs and creek.

## Stay
Caravan parks and cabins; Lazy Lizard Caravan Park listed at $40-$180 a night.

## Wildlife and culture
Gold found in 1871 by Overland Telegraph workers. Last original gold rush mining town in the Top End.

## Fuel Station
United Pine Creek and Lazy Lizard Caravan Park: diesel, LAF, P95; no unleaded 91. LAF (Opal) is the substitute for unleaded 91. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
