## Access
Gorge entrance 30 km north-east of Katherine on sealed Gorge Road.

## Hours, fees, permits
NT Parks Visitor Pass. Visitor centre hours vary; book boat tours, canoes and flights there (1300 146 743).

## Seasons and heat
Over 40 C possible October to April. Upper gorges close to swimming and canoeing during croc surveys after the wet; check status.

## Activities and swimming
Boat tours of 1 to 3 gorges. Baruwei Lookout 1.8 km return, 1 h, moderate; Baruwei Loop 4.8 km, 2 h, moderate; Butterfly Gorge 12 km return, 4.5 h, moderate; Jatbula Trail 62 km. Gorge 1 is permanently closed to swimming and canoeing.

## Stay
Nitmiluk Campground: unpowered and powered sites, cabins, pool. Cicada Lodge (luxury). Rates on operator site.

## Wildlife and culture
Jawoyn country; Nitmiluk is the Jawoyn name. Rock art sites across the park.

## Fuel Station
No fuel at the gorge. Katherine, about 30 km: diesel, LAF, P95. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
