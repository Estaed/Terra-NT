## Access
132 km west of Alice Springs on the Finke River, off Namatjira Drive. Western start of the unsealed Mereenie Loop (permit needed).

## Hours, fees, permits
NT Parks Visitor Pass for the national park.

## Seasons and heat
Over 40 C possible October to April. Cooler months are best.

## Activities and swimming
Short stroll from the car park to the gorge; swim or float in the waterhole. Views of Mount Sonder. Birds and fish.

## Stay
Glen Helen resort (25 motel rooms, powered and unpowered sites) has been reported closed: confirm it is trading before planning a night here.

## Wildlife and culture
Tjoritja / West MacDonnell National Park, Aranda country.

## Fuel Station
Glen Helen 24/7 pumps: diesel, P95. Pumps can be out of order; check MyFuel NT, else the next fuel is Alice Springs, about 130 km. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
