## Access
Unsealed, often corrugated. 4WD recommended; many hire firms ban it. Sealed 2WD alternative via Stuart, Lasseter and Luritja roads. Check NT Road Report.

## Hours, fees, permits
Crosses Aboriginal land: permit about $5 per vehicle, valid 3 days, bought in person at Alice Springs Visitor Centre, Central Land Council, Standley Chasm, Hermannsburg store, Kings Canyon Resort or Kings Creek Station.

## Seasons and heat
Cooler months best; check Road Report NT before and during the trip.

## Activities and swimming
Scenic drive only. Ginty's Lookout is the authorised stop. No swimming.

## Stay
No camping on the Loop; stop for breaks only.

## Wildlife and culture
Respect land owners' wishes. Alcohol consumption on the Loop is banned. Watch for livestock and wildlife.

## Fuel Station
No fuel on the loop. Kings Canyon Resort (diesel, ULP95) at the west end, Hermannsburg (diesel, LAF) at the east end, about 200 km apart. Fill up at both. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
