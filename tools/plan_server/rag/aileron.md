## Access
On the Stuart Highway, about 132 km north of Alice Springs and 370 km south of Tennant Creek.

## Hours, fees, permits
No entry fee. Roadhouse hours on operator site.

## Seasons and heat
Central Australian climate: hot summers; cooler months are the comfortable travel season.

## Activities and swimming
Anmatjere Man: 17 m, 8-tonne sculpture by Mark Egan, visible from the highway. Hollywood-style Aileron sign. Original Albert Namatjira watercolours in the roadhouse. Ryan Well Historical Reserve nearby. No swimming.

## Stay
Aileron Roadhouse: powered and unpowered campsites, backpacker dorms, self-contained motel rooms. Rates on operator site.

## Wildlife and culture
Anmatjere Country.

## Fuel Station
Aileron Roadhouse: diesel, LAF, P95. LAF (Opal) is the substitute for unleaded 91. Live prices and outages: MyFuel NT.
